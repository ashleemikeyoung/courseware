## Suggested order
1. Load KJV and YLT, then parallel columns and text search.
2. Matthew Henry commentary beside the text.
3. Vocabulary word clouds and proper names.
4. Cross-references and Nave's, if we find clean data.

## Data limits to know about
- The English gloss line is STEP's per-word gloss (Greek: based on the Berean Study Bible per TAGNT's header). It is not KJV or YLT.
- 551 Greek words (0.4%) have no TAGNT partner. They show unaccented Greek, with Robinson's Strong's and parsing.
- Greek transliteration is generated by the app; Hebrew transliteration comes from TAHOT.
- Greek verse numbers follow the KJV; Hebrew follows English numbering as given by TAHOT.
- Hebrew is displayed exactly as STEP stores it. Do not normalize it: NFC reorders the vowel and accent marks.
- Greek in the data uses mixed accent code points (tonos vs oxia). Any future Greek text search must normalize both the query and the text.
